# Changelog

Everything that has changed in Formelab, newest first. Shown in the admin panel under Changelog.

## 2026-11-25

- **"Use with your AI" on an app's page** (behind the "Each app's address for AI tools" feature). An app that has its own address for AI tools shows it on its page in Formelab, Sharing tab, with **Copy address** and how to add it in Claude and ChatGPT, so its owner can pass it on. The guide's **Access rules** section explains the address to AI tools building apps, and they offer a "Use with your AI" section inside the app when asked.
- **Test plan:** [Use with your AI](/test-plans/2026-11-25-use-with-your-ai), 17 browser test cases to hand to Claude Cowork.
- **Each app has its own address for AI tools** (behind the "Each app's address for AI tools" feature). Anyone who can open an app with access rules can connect Claude, ChatGPT or another AI tool to `<the app's address>/mcp`. They sign in with the app's own sign-in and approve "Connect Claude to Acme CRM?", choosing to let it change what they can or only look. That page is on formelab.ai, never on the app's own address, so nothing the app runs can press Allow for them. Their AI tool then gets tools made from the app's `formelab.json`, like "List my lead records" or "Save a lead record", and sees and changes only what their role allows: other people's records are left out, and changes they can't make are refused, the same as in the app. A role change counts on the next request, and removing someone from the app ends their connection. Apps open to everyone get general tools for all their data. Each change shows in the app's Activity under the person, with the AI tool's name. Connections to an app don't work at Formelab's own address or another app's, and don't show under Connect your AI. Files can't be published at `mcp` or `.well-known/oauth-…` while the feature is on, since the address uses them. Calls to apps' addresses aren't counted in Formelab's AI usage on the Metrics page. Also fixed: Formelab's own connection page could refuse a connection right after someone used an app's address, because the two shared a setting in memory.
- **Test plan:** [Each app's address for AI tools](/test-plans/2026-11-25-app-ai-address), 42 test cases to hand to Claude Cowork.

## 2026-11-24

- **People who only use apps are deleted when no app has them.** Someone with an account that only opens apps is deleted, sign-in and all, as soon as the last app that lists them removes them: with **Remove** under "Added in the app", with `formelab.roles.remove`, by stopping sharing with them, or by deleting the app. Their lines in the activity log stay, without their email. Formelab accounts are never deleted this way. What apps saved about them stays, for each app's owner to decide.
- **Deletion requests in the admin panel.** Under Users, **Deletion requests** finds every app that lists an email (owner, editor, shared with them, or added in the app). **Notify owners** emails each app's owner once, with a link to each app's page, so they can remove the person. The page gives the reply to send: that the request was passed to every business on Formelab that has their email, never which ones. Deleting someone's Formelab account now also takes them off the apps they were added to.
- **Test plan:** [Deleting people who use apps](/test-plans/2026-11-24-app-user-deletion), 31 browser test cases to hand to Claude Cowork.
- **Signing in to an app says the app's name.** Someone opening an app they were added to, or that's shared with them, signs in on a page that reads "Sign in to Acme CRM" with the app's address, and a small "Powered by Formelab" line, without Formelab's menu or "Join the waitlist". They don't need a Formelab account: the first time, they get an account that only opens apps. It isn't on the waitlist or counted in sign-ups, and Formelab's own pages treat it as signed out, so they never see My apps or the dashboard. If they later sign in at formelab.ai itself, they join the waitlist like anyone else, and nothing mentions the apps. Someone who signs in to an app that hasn't added them is told so, and nothing about them is kept. The admin panel shows **People using apps** apart from **People**, and marks them "uses apps" in the Users list.
- **Test plan:** [Signing in to an app](/test-plans/2026-11-24-app-sign-in), 35 browser test cases to hand to Claude Cowork.
- **Apps can give people roles themselves.** An app with access rules can now add people and give each one a role from its `formelab.json`, with `formelab.roles.set("ada@example.com", "manager")`, `formelab.roles.remove` and `formelab.roles.list`. Only the app's owner, editors and the roles in `manage_roles` can do it. Adding someone lets them open the app, even if it isn't shared with them in Formelab, and Formelab sends them nothing: the app shows a **Copy invite link** with its address instead. A role given in the app replaces `default_role` for that person, so a "manager" now sees what managers see. `formelab.me()` also says the person's `role`, and whether they manage the app. On the app's page in Formelab, people added in the app are listed under **Added in the app**, and its owner and Admins can **Remove** them. The app's Activity shows each change. The guide's **Access rules** section has a new "Members and roles" part. Signing in to an app with the app's own name comes next.
- **Test plan:** [App members and roles](/test-plans/2026-11-24-app-members-roles), 47 browser test cases to hand to Claude Cowork.
- **Access rules now cover Google Sheets and connected services.** An app's `formelab.json` can name each spreadsheet and connector it uses, under `sources`, and which roles may use it. A spreadsheet can give people only their own rows: with `"owner_column": "Assigned to"`, someone reading it gets the heading row and the rows with their email in that column, and other people's rows come back empty, so row numbers stay the same. They can add rows only with their own email in it, change only rows that are already theirs, and can't save formulas (a formula could show other rows). Custom connectors, saved keys and Gemini are "all" or "none" per role. The rules are checked on the server, so calling Formelab directly instead of through the app gets no more. A new app whose code uses Sheets or connectors can't be published until its rules say who uses them: uploading shows "This app can't be published yet" with a prompt to paste into your AI tool. Apps made before access rules work as before. Supabase works as a connector at role level for now; per-person access through Supabase's own row rules comes later. The guide's **Access rules** section explains it all.
- **Test plan:** [Access rules over outside data](/test-plans/2026-11-24-access-rules-outside-data), 63 browser test cases to hand to Claude Cowork.

## 2026-11-22

- **Access rules now apply inside apps.** In an app with a `formelab.json`, each person sees and changes only what its rules allow. People below editor have the rules' `default_role` (roles for particular people come later), and visitors from the public link have `guest`. Records someone may not see are left out of the app's lists and read as empty, as if they didn't exist, and changing a record they may not change fails with `not_allowed`. With `"own"`, people change only records that carry their own email, and can't hand them to someone else. The owner, editors and Admins still see and change everything, and each person's own data (`formelab.kv.mine`) isn't affected. For these apps the rules replace the "Viewers can add and change the app's data" switch, and the app's page says so. Apps made before access rules work as before. An app made since then that has no rules can't use saved data, Google Sheets or connectors at all, a backstop for anything the check at upload misses.
- **Test plan:** [Access rules inside apps](/test-plans/2026-11-22-access-rules-in-apps), 35 browser test cases to hand to Claude Cowork.
- **Apps without access rules say so on their page.** An app that saves data or uses connected services without a `formelab.json` now shows "**No access rules.** Everyone who can open this app can see all of its saved data, and its users can't connect their AI." at the top of its page (or that everyone who can open it can use its connected services), for its owner, editors and the editors of its space. **Add rules** opens a prompt to copy into Claude, ChatGPT or your AI tool, listing the kinds of records the app saves. Apps whose rules let people invite others show "**Treated as public.**" The warning goes away once valid rules are published. Apps made before access rules are checked the first time their page opens.
- **Test plan:** [No access rules warning](/test-plans/2026-11-22-access-rules-warning), 31 browser test cases to hand to Claude Cowork.
- **Access rules for apps that save data.** An app can now ship a `formelab.json` that says who can see and change each kind of record it saves, for example "managers see every lead, salespeople only their own", or one line, `{ "everyone": "all" }`, for polls and shared lists. New apps that save shared data (`formelab.kv`), use Google Sheets or call connectors can't be published without one: uploading shows "This app can't be published yet" with the kinds of records we found and a prompt to paste into Claude, ChatGPT or your AI tool (**Copy prompt**), and the Formelab connector gives AI tools the same reason. A broken `formelab.json` is refused for every app, with the exact problem. Rules that let people inside the app invite others (`manage_roles`) ask first: "People using this app can invite anyone. Treat it as public", with **Publish anyway**, once per app, and the app's Activity notes who accepted. Apps made before today, copies of them and apps made with the app builder keep publishing as before. The guide has a new **Access rules** section, also at `/guide`. Rules aren't applied to what people see inside an app yet; that comes next.
- **Test plan:** [Access rules on publish](/test-plans/2026-11-22-access-rules), 69 browser test cases to hand to Claude Cowork.
- **Fix: space names are checked again on the upload and Spaces forms.** The check was written in a way current browsers reject, so any name got through to the server and the console showed an error.
- **Ask your AI about an app's saved data.** The Formelab connector has four new tools, so Claude, ChatGPT and other AI tools can read and change the data an app saves with `formelab.kv`: `kv_list`, `kv_get`, `kv_set` and `kv_delete`. For example, "what's in each chafer of my Thanksgiving Menu?" now gets a real answer. Only the app's owner and editors (and the editors of its space) can use them, and only for the app's shared data: what each person keeps for themselves in `formelab.kv.mine` stays private, the owner included. Each change shows in the app's Activity under the person who asked, with the AI tool's name ("You changed "dish:2" in the app's data, via Claude"), never the value. Connections that can only look get the two reading tools. Deleting is marked as destructive, so AI tools ask first.
- **Test plan:** [Connector data tools](/test-plans/2026-11-22-connector-data-tools), 46 browser test cases to hand to Claude Cowork.
- **Fix: the login page no longer passes on the sign-in service's error messages.** When sending a code failed, the page showed WorkOS's own message, and some of those only happen for emails without an account, so anyone could tell which emails have one. Now asking for a code always shows the same "If there's an account for this email, we've sent a code" page (except when there are too many attempts), a wrong or expired code always reads "That code is invalid or expired.", and too many attempts reads "Too many attempts. Try again in a minute." everywhere. The real answer is kept in the server log. Radar's newer step names now lead to WorkOS's own sign-in page like the other extra steps.
- **Test plan:** [Sign-in answers the same for every email](/test-plans/2026-11-22-signin-enumeration), 47 browser test cases to hand to Claude Cowork.
- **Fix: the login page no longer shows who has a password.** After you enter your email, the password step used to show a tip about emailed codes only when the email had no password set, so anyone could tell which emails have a Formelab account with a password. Everyone now sees the same page, tip included.
- **Fix: people using an app can't edit its spreadsheets' structure.** People an app is shared with could make any Google Sheets call with the owner's Google account, also outside the app's own screens, including deleting tabs or clearing a sheet. They can still read values and add or change rows, which is all `formelab.sheets.read`, `append` and `update` need. Everything else (changing the spreadsheet itself, clearing, copying tabs, data filters, full grid reads) now works only for the app's owner and editors, and fails with `sheets_editors_only` for everyone else. The Google Sheets and custom connector pages now say that people who can open an app can make their own calls with its connections.

## 2026-10-24

- **One My apps page.** Shared with me is part of My apps now. Your own apps sit under **Made by you** and the apps other people shared with you under **Shared with you**, with starred apps of both kinds on top. A shared app's card shows its owner's initial on the icon, "From" and their email, and what you can do (Viewer, Editor or Admin). Search sits next to one filter button that holds every filter and the sort: **Owner** (Anyone, Me, Other people), **Space** and **Sort**. - **A page for each connector** (behind the Connected apps feature). Connected apps now shows what you've connected under **Connected** and the built-in services you haven't under **More to connect**, and each one opens its own page with the apps that use it, its settings and what happened to it. This replaces the "Where they're used" list. Google Gemini's and Google Sheets' setup moved from dialogs onto their pages.
- **Custom connectors.** **+ Custom connector** saves an API key for any service once, with the only addresses it may be sent to and how the service expects it (a Bearer header, a header of its own, a query parameter, or where the app puts `{{value}}`). Apps call it with `formelab.connectors.fetch()`, and Formelab adds the key on the way out, so it never reaches the app or the people using it. Change its settings (they save as you go), replace its key or delete it on its page. Only you can make, change or delete your connectors.
- **Apps are built to use a connector.** An app uses a connector, Google Gemini included, only once it lists it: the AI adds it with `update_site`'s new `connectors`, and only the app's owner can. Apps that had already used Gemini keep it. Remove a connector from one app on that app's Connected apps tab; editors see the list but can't change it.
- **Saved keys can become connectors.** When your apps have saved keys, Connected apps offers to turn them into connectors: apps with the same key share one, and keep working without changes. A key whose name another app uses for a different key stays in its app.
- **Test plan:** [Connectors](/test-plans/2026-10-24-connectors), 64 browser test cases to hand to Claude Cowork.
- **Connected apps on each app's page** (behind the Connected apps feature). A new **Connected apps** tab shows what the app is connected to: **Google Gemini** once the app has used it, the **Google Sheets** spreadsheets it can read and add rows to (add one by pasting its link, or remove one), and its **saved keys** by name with where each may be sent, **Replace key** and **Remove**. The keys themselves are never shown. With nothing connected, it points to the Connected apps page.
- **Where they're used.** The Connected apps page now lists which of your apps use Gemini, Google Sheets and each saved key, with links to them.
- While the Connected apps feature is off, its menu link is hidden instead of showing "Soon", and apps have no Connected apps tab.
- **Fix:** the Connected apps tab listed Google Sheets on every app once your Google Sheets was connected, even apps that don't use it. Now it only lists Sheets, and its Add a spreadsheet button, on apps that have spreadsheets. An app gets its first one from the AI that builds it.
- **Test plan:** [Settings redesign, phase 3](/test-plans/2026-10-24-settings-redesign-phase-3), 38 browser test cases to hand to Claude Cowork (its "Where they're used" cases moved to the Connectors plan).
- **Stars follow you.** Starred apps are saved to your account, so they're at the top of My apps and Shared with me on every device. Stars are yours alone: nobody else sees them. Apps you starred in this browser before today move over the first time you open My apps.
- **Editors and Admins need a Formelab account.** They sign in to make changes, so inviting someone without an account as an Editor or Admin now offers a **sign-up invite**: they get an email to join, can open the app as a Viewer right away, and become an Editor or Admin as soon as they sign up. Or add them as a Viewer instead. People without an account show "Invited, no Formelab account yet" on the People list. Editors added before today keep their access. Claude and other AI apps get the same rule when they change an app's editors.

The old owner stays on as an Admin, the app moves to their Shared with me, and they can no longer delete it, even when it's in their own space. A space holding apps you gave away can't be deleted until their new owners delete them.

- **Answers page.** Apps that collect answers with `formelab.responses` get a page at `/answers` on their own address, for their owner and editors only: every answer in a table, who sent it and when, and a spreadsheet download. Formelab adds a small **Answers** link to the app for them; it isn't linked from the app's settings. The one-line download on the Activity tab is gone.

## 2026-10-16

- **Updating a big app is much faster.** AI apps connected to Formelab no longer rewrite a whole app to change it. The publish tool now takes just the edits (find this, replace with that) or just the files that changed, and Formelab applies them to the live app. Everything else, plus the app's data, sharing and settings, stays as it is. A one-line fix to a large app takes seconds instead of minutes. If an edit doesn't match, nothing is published and the AI is told which edit and why.
- **AI apps can read every file of an app.** A new read_file tool returns any file of an app in full, in pages for very large files, so edits can be made anywhere instead of only in the first 200 KB of index.html.
- **Deploy only what changed.** `scripts/publish.sh --dir dist --changed-only` compares a folder with the live app and uploads only the files that differ, removing the ones that are gone.
- **Shared links look great.** Send an app's link in iMessage, WhatsApp, Slack, Discord, LinkedIn or anywhere else with link previews and it now shows the app's name, a line about it and a big picture of what it is, instead of "Log in to Formelab". Opening the app still needs a sign-in; only the preview is public.
- **Each app gets its own preview picture.** AI apps (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok and Make it in Formelab) now draw a share illustration for every app they make, along with a one-line description. Apps without one, including every app made before today, get a card with their icon, name and description. App makers can also add their own `share.svg` or `share.png` (1200×630), or name one with `og:image`.
- **Formelab's own links too.** formelab.ai, the login page and links into the web app show a Formelab card with "SaaS is dead." instead of a plain grey bubble.

Newer `AQ.` style keys from Google AI Studio are accepted.
